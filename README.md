# REXI (B2C)

REXI helps individuals understand, compare, and protect themselves from unfair or confusing documents before they sign or pay.

## Purpose

Most people sign documents they don’t fully understand:
- Job offer letters
- Insurance policies
- Loan agreements
- Rental agreements
- Subscription contracts

These documents are long, legal, and intentionally complex. REXI converts them into simple, structured truth.

## Core B2C Use Case

**Upload a document → REXI explains what it really says, what’s risky, and what’s normal.**

No legal jargon. No guessing.

## What Problems REXI Solves

**Without REXI:**
- People don’t notice hidden clauses
- Insurance sounds “guaranteed” but returns are terrible
- Offer letters look higher but cost-of-living makes them worse
- Renewal traps and penalties go unnoticed
- People rely on agents or Google guesses

**With REXI:**
- Plain-English explanation of any document
- Side-by-side comparison (Offer A vs Offer B, Policy A vs Policy B)
- Highlights unfair or risky clauses
- Shows “what this means for you” clearly

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. Offer Letter Analysis & Comparison
- Breaks salary, bonuses, notice period, non-compete
- Adjusts for city cost-of-living
- Shows which offer is actually better, not just higher CTC
- *Example:* “Offer B pays ₹1L more but costs ₹22K/month extra to live in Bangalore.”

### 2. Insurance Policy Understanding
- Converts policy PDFs into a simple dashboard:
    - What’s covered
    - What’s excluded
    - Waiting periods
    - Lock-ins
- Flags misleading “guaranteed” language
- Shows real IRR / value, not marketing promises

### 3. Contract Risk Detection
- Rental agreements
- Subscription terms
- Service contracts
- *Example:* “This contract auto-renews and allows price increases without notice.”
