This policy offers extensive coverage for your bike against natural calamities, theft, fire, and accidents. It also covers you from third party liabilities.

## THIRD-PARTY INSURANCE

ACKO third-party two-wheeler insurance protects you against damage or loss to third-party property, vehicle, or physical injury to a person.
